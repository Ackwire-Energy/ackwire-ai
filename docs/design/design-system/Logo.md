# Logo

The brand is **Ackwire AI**, following the Ackwire Design System (Ackwire Energy Brand Guidelines 2026). The logo is the Ackwire "A" monogram beside the words "Ackwire AI": "Ackwire" in the ink, "AI" in the accent, bold with tight tracking (Gilroy ExtraBold in the brand; the app's sans stands in). The "A" and "AI" use `agent`: Solar Green `#90ED42` on dark, its deep shade `#3F7A12` on light, where Solar Green fails contrast. One-color version: `ac-logo--mono` (all in the ink). Below 18px, or in a square space (favicon, app icon, a workspace without a logo), use the "A" alone (`LogoMark`). The app icon and favicon are the Solar Green "A" on a Forest Green `#031F1B` tile. The monogram's path and names live in `packages/ui/src/brand/ackwire.ts`. Never stretch, recolor outside the accent or ink, or add effects to the logo.

Colors: Ackwire's greens are accents and branding only. Backgrounds stay neutral: near white on light, black on dark (never Forest Green).
