# 0083. Groups of agents

- Status: Accepted. Amended 2026-10-30: §4 and §5 (a person's lists show only the agents that answer them, unless they manage agents)
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Code: `packages/db/src/schema/agents.ts` (`agent_groups`, `agents.group_id`, `agents.group_position`), `packages/engine/src/agent-groups.ts`, `apps/server/src/routes/app/agents.ts`, `packages/client/src/lib/agent-groups.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/AgentGroups.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/TeamGroups.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/agents/AgentsScreen.tsx`

## Context

A workspace with many agents (an import brings twenty or more, for several companies and projects) had one long "Agentes" list in the sidebar. Other agent apps show their agents in titled sections. Nothing here grouped agents, and channels have no sections either, so there was no pattern to follow.

## Decision

1. **Groups belong to the workspace** and everyone in it sees them, like channels. A group has a name (up to 80 characters) and a place among the groups, at most 50 of them. An agent is in one group at most: its group is a column of the agent (`agents.group_id`, with its place inside, `group_position`), so the database holds that rule. Deleting a group sets its agents back to no group (the foreign key does it).
2. **Owners and admins change them** (`manage_agents`, as for the agents themselves). Members read them and close the ones they don't need, on their own screen.
3. **One view for every app.** `GET …/agent-groups` gives the groups in order, each with its agents in order, archived ones left out. Every change (`POST …/agent-groups`, `PATCH` and `DELETE …/agent-groups/{groupId}`, `PUT …/agents/{agentId}/group` with a place inside the group) answers with that whole list and tells the workspace's open apps with the org event `agent.groups`, on which each app reads the list again. The event carries no list: the list grows with the agents (fifty groups of long names, or a couple hundred agents in groups), and Postgres NOTIFY, which carries org events between replicas, refuses payloads near 8000 bytes. Agents in no group keep the workspace's order (when they were created). The refusals a person can meet carry codes the apps translate: `agent_group.too_many` (with `max`) and `agent_group.not_found` (a group someone else deleted meanwhile).
4. **Web and desktop app.** The agents in no group stay under "Agentes", and each group is a category after it, which closes like the others (remembered in the browser). For owners and admins: "+" on "Agentes" makes a group; "…" on a group renames it, moves it up or down, or deletes it after a question; "…" on an agent's row, shown on hover, picks its group, makes a new one with it, or moves it up or down inside its group; and rows drag between categories, to a place inside a group. The agent's page has a Grupo field in Configurações › Identidade, saved with the rest of the page and shown once the workspace has groups. A workspace with no groups sees the sidebar as before. *Amended 2026-10-30:* the sidebar lists only the agents the person can talk to, from the server's `canTalk` (the agent's audience and `talk_to_agents`, ADR 0085; `listsAgent` in `packages/client/src/lib/audience.ts`, so a later limit per person reaches every list unchanged), and a group with none of those doesn't show for them. The same rule holds in their @ suggestions, ⌘K and agent pickers (Criar canal, adding to a channel, a task's assignee). An empty channel's suggestions, its "/" commands and the mission they open go, for everyone, to an agent that answers the person. Who manages agents (`manage_agents`) sees every agent, a locked one with the lock that says who it answers, and every group, empty ones too: they arrange them. Equipe stays the directory, every agent for everyone, and messages agents already posted stay as they are. *Amended 2026-11-02:* the sidebar no longer lists agents (its "Mensagens diretas" part lists conversations, people and agents together). The same arrangement moved to Equipe's "Grupos de agentes" card (`TeamGroups.tsx`): the agents in no group under "Agentes" and every group for who manages agents, who also make, rename, order and delete groups and move agents there by the "…" (always shown, touch screens too) or by dragging; everyone else reads the groups holding an agent they can talk to.
5. **Phone.** "Esperando você" and "Trabalhando agora" stay first. "Livres" splits into the agents in no group ("Sem grupo" once there are groups) and one section per group, which collapses and is remembered on the phone, on one list for every workspace (group ids are unique). Agents move between groups on the web for now. *Amended 2026-10-30:* for someone who doesn't manage agents, Agentes, the home's greeting (who works, who rests) and its Agentes (the DMs with agents, with the tab's badge) and adding to a channel leave out the agents that don't answer them, by the same rule as the web. The home waits for the agents before it shows, so those DMs don't flash in. Someone whom no agent answers reads that on Agentes, not "no agents yet".
6. **Imports.** `createAgentGroup(deps, userId, orgId, { name, agentIds })` makes a group after the others with these agents in it, in order, taking them out of any other group. An importer that brings sections of agents calls it once per section.

## Consequences

- Changes to groups are not in the audit log, like the board's columns: they change how lists read, not what an agent may do.
- An archived agent keeps its group and is out of every view while archived.
- Touch screens show no "…" on a row (there is no hover): there, the agent's page moves an agent.
- Two moves at the same time may give two agents the same place in a group. The order then falls back on when they were created, and the next move numbers the group again.
- An import run twice makes its groups twice unless the importer looks for a group of the same name first (`listAgentGroups`).
